# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Blueprint Chart to be a welcoming, friendly, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level. Maintainers and contributors pledge to make participation a positive experience for all.

## Our standards

We expect everyone in the community to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and welcoming.
- Assume good intent and offer constructive, specific feedback.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Behaviour that harms others or makes the community unwelcoming is not acceptable. This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as the full statement of expected and unacceptable behaviour and of enforcement responsibilities. Please read it; it is the authoritative standard for this project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the project maintainer at **conduct@blueprintchart.com**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and safety will be respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take any action they deem appropriate, up to and including a temporary or permanent ban from the project, in response to behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct. Enforcement follows the guidelines described in the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
